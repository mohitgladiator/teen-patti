Official Teen Patti Hand Ranking Rules

1. Trio / Trail (Three of a Kind)

Three cards of the same rank. A-A-A is the highest possible hand in Teen Patti, down to 2-2-2 as the lowest trail.

2. Pure Sequence (Straight Flush)

Three consecutive cards of the same suit. A-2-3 is the highest Pure Sequence, followed by A-K-Q, K-Q-J, down to 4-3-2.

3. Sequence / Straight (Run)

Three consecutive cards of mixed suits. Like pure sequence, A-2-3 ranks highest, then A-K-Q down to 4-3-2.

4. Flush / Color

Three cards of the same suit that are not in sequence. High card breaks ties (e.g., A-J-9 beats A-10-8).

5. Pair (Double)

Two cards of equal rank. Pair of Aces (A-A-x) is highest. If pair ranks match, the highest kicker card determines the winner.

6. High Card

Three cards that fit none of the above categories. Highest card ranks top (Ace high beats King high).
